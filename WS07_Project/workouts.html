My Workout Split

This push–pull–legs split helped me achieve most of my gains. I trained six days per week with one rest day. Every exercise was performed for 3 sets in the 8–12 rep range.

Push Day

  • Bench Press
  • Incline Dumbbell Press
  • Pec Fly
  • Tricep Extensions
  • Shoulder Press
  • Lateral Raises

Pull Day

  • Pull-Ups
  • Seated Row
  • T-Bar Row
  • Rear Delts
  • Bicep Curls
  • Shrugs

Leg Day

  • Hack Squats
  • Leg Extensions
  • Hamstring Curls
  • Calf Raises
  • Abs Machine
